Word Bridge

Lexi needs to cross the gorge, and the bridge is your sentence. Each word is a plank. Pick the words in the right order, but watch out: some words do not belong.

Words that go together hold fast. Where the grammar is wrong, the bridge snaps under Lexi's weight.

Click a word or press its number. Backspace takes the last word off.

Bet on it. Not sure, Quite sure or Certain: bigger bets win more and lose more, so bet what you really think. After each bridge, bank your points or push on for a bigger multiplier.

The Troll hides trap words in the grammar you find hardest. Press H to Scout and remove one. You have 3 hearts, and 3 bridges reach the next world.

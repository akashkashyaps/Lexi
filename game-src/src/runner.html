Grammar Runner

Lexi the fox runs toward three gates. Each gate has a word. A sentence with a gap shows at the top of the screen. Move into the lane with the word that fits the gap. The sentence shows your word in the gap, so you can read it before you get there.

← →Change lane (or press 1, 2, 3) ↑ holdRun faster when you are sure PPause

5 right answers open the portal to the next world. You have 3 hearts. Each wrong answer costs one, and each new world gives one back.
